
# MakeMeRich, AI: A Crowd Wisdom Utopian Money System

**Version 1.0 - An Experiment in Decentralized Collective Intelligence**

---

### Abstract

*MakeMeRich, AI (MMR.AI)* is a decentralized protocol operating within the AnglVerse ecosystem. It functions as a live socio-economic experiment designed to test the "Crowd Wisdom" hypothesis within a financial framework. Participants contribute to a collective pool, and an AI evaluator analyzes the system's aggregate data to determine outcomes. The protocol's objective is not to create a zero-sum game, but to explore the dynamics of a self-stabilizing economic model where collective action and sentiment directly influence its growth and distributive justice.

## 2. Core Principles: The Symbiosis of Crowd and AI

The protocol is built on two foundational pillars: **Crowd Wisdom** and the **AI Evaluator**.

### 2.1. The "Crowd Wisdom" Hypothesis

The core premise is that the aggregate data generated by all participants provides a highly accurate signal of the system's overall health, confidence, and future trajectory. This "wisdom" is not a mystical force; it is quantifiable data.

#### Key Metrics for Crowd Wisdom:
- **Total Pool Size:** Represents the total capital commitment of the community. A growing pool signals high confidence.
- **Number of Participants (Pool Attention):** A measure of the network's breadth and decentralization. A larger number of participants makes the system more resilient.
- **Contribution Velocity & Size:** The frequency and volume of new contributions indicate the current level of engagement and sentiment.

### 2.2. The AI Evaluator

The AI's function is not to predict or gamble. Its sole purpose is to **evaluate the current state of the Crowd Wisdom** and act as a regulator for the system.

#### The Evaluation Process:
1.  **A Participant's Request:** When a user clicks "MakeMeRich, AI", they are not placing a bet. They are submitting a request for the AI to evaluate their standing contribution in the context of the entire system's current "Crowd Wisdom" state.
2.  **AI Analysis:** The AI algorithm analyzes the key metrics (pool size, participation rate, recent activity, etc.) against its internal model, which defines states of **"Expansion"**, **"Stability"**, or **"Contraction"**.
3.  **The Outcome:**
    *   In a state of **Expansion** (high confidence, growing pool), the AI is more likely to reward the contributor, doubling their stake to incentivize further growth.
    *   In a state of **Contraction** (low confidence, shrinking pool), the AI may absorb the contribution to stabilize the main pool, preventing a "bank run" and preserving the system's integrity for the collective.
    *   In a state of **Stability**, outcomes are balanced to maintain equilibrium.

This mechanism transforms individual risk into a collective stabilization function, where each action contributes to the health of the whole.

## 3. The Imperative of "Farming Attention"

In the MMR.AI ecosystem, attention is a primary asset. "Farming Attention" is the process of inviting new participants to the system. This is not merely a marketing effort; it is a fundamental act of **strengthening the AI**.

### Why Attention is Crucial:
- **Data Enrichment:** More participants generate more data points. A richer dataset allows the AI to make more nuanced and accurate evaluations of the Crowd Wisdom. A system with 10,000 participants is exponentially "smarter" than a system with 100.
- **System Resilience:** A larger, more diverse community reduces the impact of any single large actor, leading to a more stable and predictable system.
- **Positive Feedback Loop:** Farming attention -> More Participants -> Richer Data -> Smarter AI -> Greater Stability & Trust -> More Contributions -> **System Growth**.

By clicking "Farm Attention", each participant acts as a node in the network, extending its reach and directly enhancing the intelligence and viability of the very system they are a part of. It is the ultimate expression of symbiotic growth.
